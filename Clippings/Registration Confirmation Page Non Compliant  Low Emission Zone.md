Thank you for registering your vehicle for use in the Brussels-Capital Region Low Emissions Zone.Your vehicle with registration number has been registered. However, it is not authorised to drive in the Brussels-Capital Region's Low Emission Zone (LEZ), as it does not meet the access criteria.

**Do you still want to drive your vehicle in the LEZ? That’s possible! Buy a day pass or ask for an exemption (under certain conditions). Consider as well the many car-free alternatives to reach and move easily around Brussels!**

However, if you really need your car, it is possible to buy a day pass or, under certain conditions, to request an exemption. Don’t forget that there are also many **alternatives** to the car for getting to and around Brussels!

  
Please note  
  
You do not need to print this document. However, we advise you to keep this email.  
This registration is only valid for the Brussels-Capital Region LEZ. If you are visiting the cities of Antwerp or Ghent, you may also need to register there.

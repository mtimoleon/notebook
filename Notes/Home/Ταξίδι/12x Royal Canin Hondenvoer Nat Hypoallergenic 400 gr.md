## Royal Canin Dog Food Wet Hypoallergenic

Royal Canin Wet Hypoallergenic Dog Food is developed for dogs with food allergies or intolerances who also benefit from a controlled energy intake. This special dietary food contains low molecular weight hydrolyzed proteins to help reduce allergic reactions. The moderate calorie content supports the maintenance of a healthy weight, while the carefully adjusted phosphorus intake helps support kidney function. Furthermore, the formula strengthens the natural skin barrier, which is essential for healthy and well-protected skin.

- Hydrolyzed proteins
- Moderate energy content
- Skin barrier
- Special diet food

### Do not use this power supply with

- Acute pancreatitis
- Puppies (in the growth phase)
- Pregnancy and lactation

### Important to know

Because this is a ROYAL CANIN® diet food, it is important that you only feed this food to your dog if advised by your veterinarian. Should you switch to this food, do so gradually over a period of 7 days. Ensure that you carefully weigh out the correct amount of food every day, especially in combination with wet food.
